# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

plain HTML/CSS; delegated: lightweight vanilla JavaScript may be used for interactive route, countdown, language, and navigation behavior.

## Users

Mountain bike riders in Chile, especially riders considering travel to Valdivia, plus supporters, families, and visitors looking for event information and race results.

## Product Purpose

Valdivia Bike Fest is the public information and conversion site for a mountain bike race in Valdivia, Chile. It must explain the event, route, schedule, and results, and send prospective riders to the existing external registration platform. Success means qualified visitors understand the event and reach the registration platform.

## Positioning

The site connects a real Valdivian riding experience with the practical information needed to commit: route, timing, registration, and results in one bilingual event home.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive from search, social media, or event sharing on mobile. They need to scan while planning travel, compare route details, check timing, and later return for results. Registration is completed on an existing external platform; this site does not process registrations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Main information areas: Inicio, Evento, Ruta y Horario, Resultados, Valdivia, and Contacto.
- Inicio does not need to appear as a primary navigation item.
- Primary call to action: "Inscríbete"; it redirects to the existing registration platform.
- Required functionality: interactive route exploration, countdown, social integration, results display, and bilingual content.
- SEO targets: Valdivia Bike Fest, competencia mountain bike Valdivia, inscripciones mountain bike Valdivia, eventos mountain bike Chile.
- The site must be plain HTML/CSS with lightweight client-side behavior and preserve external links/assets supplied by the user.

## Brand Commitments

- Primary turquoise: `#52C7B4`.
- Deep brand blue-green: `#4C6444`.
- White: `#F7F7F7`.
- Black: `#1A1A1A`.
- Use `img/logo cropped.png` as the current cropped logo placeholder until a production logo is supplied.
- Use the supplied imagery in `/img` as the approved visual asset source.

## Evidence on Hand

The user has real brand and event assets to provide. No asset paths or final event data are present in the repository yet; synthetic content must be clearly replaceable and must not be presented as verified commercial claims.

## Product Principles

- Make the decision to ride feel immediate and grounded in place.
- Put registration, route, and timing within seconds of arrival.
- Treat practical race information as part of the experience, not an afterthought.
- Serve Spanish-first visitors and English-speaking visitors with equal clarity.
- Never invent commercial facts, race results, or event specifics that have not been supplied.
